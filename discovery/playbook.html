HisaabAI interview playbook

Read once on Friday night. Carry it folded behind the interview sheets. Goal for the weekend: 15 honest conversations, not 15 yeses.

1. Before you go

  • When: 2 to 5 pm. Never at opening, lunch rush or evening rush. Leave if a customer walks in; come back.
  • Carry: 15 printed sheets, 2 pens, phone charged (bill photos), water. Simple clothes; no laptop bag "pitch" look.
  • Plan: visit 8 shops a day to get about 5 conversations. Mix: kirana/general, medical, hardware/sanitary; one-person and 3+ staff shops.
  • Never: anyone linked to BASIC Home Loan or your job.

2. Opening line (first 10 seconds)

Say: "Namaste ji. Main ek chhota project bana raha hoon, ye samajhne ke liye ki dukaan wale supplier ke bill aur hisaab kaise sambhalte hain. Kuch bechne nahi aaya. Jab customer na ho, 15 minute baat kar sakte hain?"
  • Then stop talking and wait. Silence is fine.
  • Start with an easy question (Q1). Ask about their shop, not your idea.

3. When they push back

"Kya bechna hai?""Kuch nahi. Abhi sirf samajh raha hoon. Agar aage kuch banega, toh pehle aapko free mein dikhaunga."
"Time nahi hai""Koi baat nahi. Bas 3 sawaal, 5 minute? Ya kis waqt aaun?" Then use the 5-minute version below.
"Hisaab CA dekhta hai"Good answer, keep going: "CA tak bill kaise pahunchte hain? Mahine mein kitni baar? Beech mein kuch gum hota hai?"
"Sab theek chal raha hai""Bahut badhiya! Pichhli baar supplier ke saath hisaab mein gadbad kab hui thi?" A real story usually follows.
"Naam kyun chahiye?""Naam ya number kahin nahi likhunga. Bas S01, S02 jaisa number." Show the sheet.
"Aap kaun ho?"Be honest: your name, that you work in Gurugram and are building this on your own. Never claim a company, a bank or a government scheme.

4. Asking for bill photos (after Q14)

Say: "Ek last cheez. Purane, paid ho chuke bills ki 5 se 10 photo le sakta hoon? Taaki dekh sakoon system unhe padh pata hai ya nahi. Naam main baad mein chhupa dunga, ya aap ungli rakh dijiye."
  • Only supplier bills. Never photograph the udhaar register or anything with customer names.
  • Mix printed and handwritten (kachcha) bills; that mix is what the eval needs.
  • Flat on the counter, in daylight, whole bill in frame. One bill per photo.
  • If they say no: "Bilkul theek hai." Thank them, no second ask. Get the consent slip signed (or say "recorded verbally").

5. Real pain vs polite "haan"

Signs it is real

  • They tell a specific recent story with a number ("pichhle hafte 3,000 ka bill do baar pay ho gaya").
  • They show you the bill pile or register without being asked.
  • They already built a workaround: a diary, an Excel file, a son who helps on Sundays.
  • They ask you a question back: "kab milega?", "kitne ka hoga?"
  • They give their number for the trial without hesitation.

Signs it is politeness

  • "Haan haan, accha idea hai" with no story behind it.
  • "Dekhenge", "baad mein batayenge".
  • They talk about other shops' problems, not their own.
  • Only future tense ("use karenge"), never past ("pichhli baar...").
  • Agree to try but avoid giving a number.

Commitment test (end of Q13): "Pilot shuru hone par aapko WhatsApp pe add kar doon? Number de dijiye." A number = real yes. Write only the Shop ID on the sheet; the number goes in discovery/private/shops_tracker.csv.

6. Do and don't

Do

  • Ask "pichhli baar kya hua?" and "dikha sakte hain?"
  • Write their exact words for every pain.
  • Time them recording 3 bills (Q5): the "before" number for the CV.
  • Follow a surprise. If udhaar or cash is the bigger pain, dig there.

Don't

  • Describe the bot before Q13. It turns their answers into compliments.
  • Ask leading questions ("bills sambhalna mushkil hai na?").
  • Argue, correct them, or promise dates, prices or "free forever".
  • Stay over 25 minutes, or stay when customers arrive.

7. The 5-minute version (busy shops)

Ask only Q2 (bills per week), Q6 (last missed or late payment), Q10 (biggest headache), Q13 (try for 2 weeks + number) and Q15 (bill photos). Mark the sheet "SHORT".

8. After each shop (within 1 hour)

  • Fill page 2 of the sheet: wedge scores 1 to 5, best quote, surprise.
  • Add a row to discovery/private/shops_tracker.csv on the laptop that evening.
  • Photos go to data/raw/bills/, named with the Shop ID (S03_1.jpg ...).

9. End of Saturday check

  • Count how many owners chose each wedge as the biggest headache (Q10).
  • If most point away from supplier bills, tell Claude on Saturday night. Sunday's questions can then dig deeper into that pain.
  • Target by Sunday: 15 conversations, 100+ bill photos, 5+ numbers for the trial.

10. Trust and pitch (only after Q13, never before)

Ladder of asks. Climb one step at a time. Stop at the step where they hesitate, thank them, and come back another day.

StepAskWhy it is easy to say yes
110-minute talk about how they handle billsCosts nothing, nothing shared
2Photos of 3-5 old, paid supplier billsOld bills are low risk; they can cover names with a finger
3Watch you send one bill to the bot on your phoneThey see the result before anything touches their phone
42-week free trial on their own WhatsAppThey have already seen it work, and STOP ends it any time

11. The 20-second pitch

Say: "Supplier ka bill aaye toh bas uski photo WhatsApp pe bhej dijiye. Ye supplier ka naam aur total padh ke aapse poochhega 'sahi hai?'. Aap haan bolo, toh hisaab mein jud jaata hai. 'Kitna baaki hai' likho toh har supplier ka baaki bata dega, aur due date se 2 din pehle yaad dila dega. Register mein likhne ki zarurat nahi. 2 hafte free try karke dekhiye, pasand na aaye toh STOP likh dijiye."

12. What to promise (all true; never promise more)

  • Only you see it. Your bills and hisaab are not shared with anyone, not with suppliers, banks, loan companies or the government.
  • A computer reads the photo, not a person. An AI service reads the bill. It does not use the photo to train itself.
  • Photos are deleted after 30 days. Only the numbers stay (supplier, total, date). Exception: if you say yes to helping test bill reading, those photos are kept for testing, with names hidden.
  • You can skip photos. Type the bill instead: "Sharma Traders 4580".
  • You can stop any time. STOP ends messages. Ask me and I delete your whole data.
  • Nothing to buy, no loan, no app. It is a student-style project by me, built on my own, not by a bank or company.

13. Trust objections

"Photo kahan jaayegi?""Ek computer program padhta hai, koi insaan nahi. 30 din mein photo delete, sirf total aur supplier ka naam rehta hai. Chahein toh photo mat bhejiye, likh dijiye."
"Data bechoge?""Nahi. Na bechna hai, na kisi ko dikhana hai. Aap kehenge toh sab delete kar dunga, aapke saamne."
"GST / income tax wale ko pata chalega?""Bilkul nahi. Ye kisi sarkari system se juda nahi hai. Sirf aapke supplier bills ka hisaab hai, aapke liye."
"Supplier ko pata chalega?""Nahi. Bot sirf aapko message karta hai, kisi aur ko kabhi nahi."
"Free kyun hai?""Abhi main seekh raha hoon ki ye kaam ka hai ya nahi. Aapka feedback hi meri fees hai." (Do not promise "free forever".)
"Galat padh liya toh?""Har bill pe pehle aapse poochhta hai 'sahi hai?'. Galat ho toh 'Badlo' dabaiye aur sahi likh dijiye. Aapke haan ke bina kuch nahi judta."
"Khatabook toh hai""Bahut badhiya. Usmein supplier bill khud type karna padta hai? Ye photo se karta hai. 2 hafte dono saath chala ke dekhiye."
"Beta / munim karta hai""Unse bhi baat kar sakta hoon? Number unka bhi chalega." Ask who actually records the bills.

Realistic yes-rate: roughly 1 trial yes for every 3 real conversations. A "no" after step 2 still gives bill photos for the eval, so it is not a wasted visit.
