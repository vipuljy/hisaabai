# Outreach kit (Phase 1, remote-first)

Copy-paste ready. Honest, short, no selling. Never forward anyone's number without asking them first.

## 1. WhatsApp group post (referrals + bills + poll)

```
Hi all, a small ask 🙏

I'm building HisaabAI, a WhatsApp tool for small shop owners (kirana, medical, hardware). They send a photo of a supplier bill, and it keeps their hisaab and reminds them before payments are due.

3 ways you can help:
1. Intro: if someone in your family or circle runs a shop, could you connect me for a 10 minute call? I'm not selling anything, only learning how they handle bills today.
2. Bill photos: if you have supplier bills or shop bills (printed or handwritten), please send me 2-3 photos. I use them only to test bill reading, and hide names before anyone else sees them.
3. Poll: if you run a business yourself, please vote in the poll below. Takes 10 seconds.

Thank you!
```

## 2. Polls (WhatsApp: attach > Poll, "Allow multiple answers" ON)

**Poll 1:** `Business owners: what is the biggest hassle in your hisaab? (pick up to 2)`
- Supplier bills and paying suppliers on time
- Getting customers to pay back (udhaar)
- Matching daily cash and UPI
- GST and filings
- Finding time to write everything down
- No real hassle

**Poll 2:** `How do you keep track of supplier bills today?`
- Register or diary
- Excel or Google Sheets
- An app (Khatabook, Vyapar, Tally...)
- CA or accountant handles it
- I don't track them

Note: these groups are mostly founders and online sellers, not kirana shops. Report their answers as a separate segment.

## 3. Intro message (send yourself, or ask your friend to forward)

```
Namaste [naam] ji, main Vipul, [dost ka naam] ka dost. Main chhoti dukaanon ke liye ek WhatsApp tool bana raha hoon jo supplier ke bill ka hisaab apne aap rakhe. Kuch bechna nahi hai. Bas 10 minute phone pe samajhna hai ki aap abhi bills aur payments kaise sambhalte hain. Aapko kab theek rahega?
```

## 4. Bill photo follow-up (after someone says yes)

```
Thank you! Please send 2-3 photos of any supplier or shop bills: one bill per photo, flat, in daylight, full bill visible. Old paid bills are perfect. Handwritten (kachcha) bills help the most. I'll hide all names and use them only to test bill reading.
```

## 5. Phone or WhatsApp-call interviews

- Use the same `interview_sheet.html`; it works over a call. Ask "WhatsApp pe 2-3 bill ki photo bhej sakte hain?" instead of taking photos yourself.
- Ask Q5 as "Pichhla bill likhne mein kitna time laga tha?" since you cannot time them.
- Consent: read the consent line aloud and note "recorded verbally" with the date.
- Calls of 10-15 minutes at 2-5 pm or after 9 pm work best for shop owners.

## 6. Optional: Google Form (if you prefer a link to polls)
1. What do you sell? (kirana / medical / hardware / online / other)
2. Supplier bills per week? (0-5 / 6-20 / 21-50 / 50+)
3. When did a supplier payment last go late or get missed? (this month / last 3 months / longer ago / never)
4. Biggest hassle in your hisaab? (same options as Poll 1)
5. Would you try a free WhatsApp tool that reads bill photos, for 2 weeks? (Yes / Maybe / No)
6. Okay to call you for 10 minutes? Phone number (optional)

Answers with phone numbers are private: export them only into `discovery/private/`.

## Tracking
Log every conversation in `discovery/private/shops_tracker.csv` with a channel in `notes` (warm call, group referral, walk-in), so the case study can say where users came from.
