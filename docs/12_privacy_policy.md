# Vahi – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026
**Operator:** VAHI, a sole proprietorship registered in India (Udyam: UDYAM-HR-05-0212100), Gurugram, Haryana
**Contact:** vypassionett@gmail.com

Vahi is a WhatsApp assistant that helps small shop owners keep track of supplier bills and payments. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

## 1. What we collect
- Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send to Vahi.
- Bill photos and voice notes you choose to send.
- Details read from your bills (supplier name, total, bill date, invoice number, due date) and the payments you record.
- Your shop's name, if you tell us. Your WhatsApp profile name is used only to suggest it (you confirm or change it) and is not stored.

We do not collect contacts, location, or any information you do not send to Vahi.

## 2. Why we use it
- To keep your supplier ledger: bills, payments and amounts due.
- To send reminders before a payment is due and a weekly summary (you can turn these off with STOP).
- To improve how accurately Vahi reads bills.

## 3. Who processes it
- **Meta (WhatsApp)** delivers messages between you and Vahi.
- **Anthropic** (AI service) reads bill photos and some voice-note text to pull out the bill details. Your data is not used to train its models.
- **Sarvam AI** converts voice notes to text.
- **Supabase** stores the data securely in the cloud.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, including suppliers, banks, lenders or government bodies, unless the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it
- **Bill photos are deleted after 30 days.** Only the details read from them are kept. The exception is if you have explicitly agreed to let us keep photos to test reading accuracy.
- Ledger data is kept while you use Vahi.

## 5. Your choices and data deletion
- Send **STOP** at any time to stop all messages.
- STOP keeps your hisaab safe, so START brings it back.
- **To delete all your data:** send "Mera data delete karo" (or DELETE) to Vahi on WhatsApp, also after STOP. Vahi shows what will be deleted and asks once; on "Haan, sab mitao" your shop, bills, payments, messages, reminders and photos are deleted **at once**. Or email vypassionett@gmail.com from any address with your WhatsApp number, and we delete it within **48 hours** and confirm when done.

## 6. Security
Access to the data is limited to the operator. Phone numbers are shortened in logs, the database is protected with row-level security, and photos are kept in a private store.

## 7. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this link with a new effective date.

This policy follows the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: clear purpose, consent, and deletion on request.
