# Guardrails, understanding and trust (3 Nov 2026)

Built after Vipul's second phone test (2 Nov). This doc lists every doubt from that test with what the bot did before
and what it does now, the design rules behind the fixes, and what was measured. The chats themselves:
`docs/16_voice_typed_entries.md` (regenerated by `python scripts/entry_demo.py`).

## 2. Design rules (the product thinking behind the fixes)
1. **Nothing counts before the tap.** AI reads, the owner decides. Every guard is a line on a card, not a block.
2. **Show the consequence, not the form.** One line, "Baaki: ₹5,000 → ₹3,000", lets the owner check the number they
   already know instead of re-reading every field.
3. **Make guesses visible.** If the bot picked "Sharma Traders" from "Sharma", the card says so.
4. **Ask only when the answer changes the ledger.** One question at a time, buttons not typing, the likely answer first
   (a reminder first when the amount is already owed; read the photo before asking about proof).
5. **Warn, don't forbid.** A real second payment of the same amount is one tap away ("Naya hai, save").
6. **Never rewrite history.** Edits only in the last 7 days, each kept in `entry_edits`; older mistakes get a dated
   correcting entry; removal is a status, never an erase. The only true erase is the owner's own "delete my data".
7. **A wrong card is the metric.** A missed message is safe (the owner is asked); a confident wrong card is not. Every
   eval counts WRONG separately, and it must stay 0.
8. **A narrow fix beats a clever one.** Spelling slips are fixed only for meaning words, never for names; a wrong
   "correction" would be worse than none.
9. **Privacy by default.** Photos deleted after 30 days, the WhatsApp profile name never stored, one message to delete
   everything.
10. **Measure, then claim.** Every number in this doc comes from a script in the repo.

## 3. How it is built
- `bot/understand.py` (new): Hindi script → Roman letters (letter rules, the silent 'a': कितना → kitna, loan words:
  पेमेंट → payment; where a vowel has two spellings the one the rules know is picked); spelling slips
  (Damerau-Levenshtein, 1 letter, 2 for 7+ letters, same first letter, a consonant-skeleton rule only for "hisaab").
  Runs before both engines, so a word means the same in entries and questions.
- `bot/entries.py`: old-dues phrases, "se ___ liya" bills, the refund reason from the words, "ne galti se zyada liye
  the, 300 wapas kiye" is a refund (not a fix).
- `bot/entry_flow.py`: `_card_checks` (before → after, duplicate, usual amount, advance, date ahead), `_card_buttons`,
  the old-dues question, refund pairs, dated corrections (`_older_matches`, `_offer_adjustment`), photo-first proof
  (`_proof_match`, `_proof_from_text`), stale-button answers (`_gone`), the per-message cache (`_memo`).
- `bot/conversation.py`: the intro with header / footer, "mera data delete karo", reply timing, the cleaned text.
- `bot/store.py` (both stores): `reason` on bills and payments, `proofless_bills`, `latest_photo_bill`,
  `detach_photo`, `bill_by_invoice`, `paired`, `shop_counts`, per-kind supplier summary, `get_entry` as one query, shop
  codes never repeat while another shop holds them.
- `db/schema.sql`: `reason` columns (run `RUN_setup_database.bat` once; it is safe to re-run).

## 6. Second phone test (16 Nov): findings and fixes

Rounds 11-17 of the phone guide, on the laptop bot. Four findings, all reproduced first, then fixed with tests.

| # | Finding | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "gupta gave me 500" became a ₹500 **bill** for "Gupta Gave"; only the duplicate warning stopped it. Checking more English found three more wrong cards: "gupta paid me 500" (a payment BY the shop), "returned goods 500 to gupta" (a bill), and in Hinglish "gupta ne maal wapas liya 300 ka" (money back, when the goods went back) | The rules knew Hinglish, not English verbs; the unknown word joined the name, and "name + amount" is a bill | `_english()` in `bot/entries.py`: gave / paid / sent / got / received / bought / returned / took back / owe, with me / to / from, used only when the message has no Hinglish grammar word. English verbs never make the "name + amount" bill shape and are never part of a name. "X ne maal wapas liya" is a return |
| 2 | "Gupta se 500 mile vapas" + "Maal lautaya tha", for goods bought on credit: the card kept the baaki at ₹500. "If the goods went back, why would he pay me? It cancels out" | The bot took "500 mile" literally: cash came in | Goods back while the shop still owes at least that much: one question, "Paisa haath mein mila, ya baaki se kata?" [✂️ Baaki se kata] (first, the usual case: a return, the baaki goes down) [💵 Paisa mila] (return + refund, the baaki stays). Nothing owed (already paid): cash back makes sense, no question |
| 3 | "Took ₹500 of goods, returned them, he accepted only ₹300: what then?" | Two amounts in one message were not understood ("300 hi kata" became a supplier called "Kata") | Two amounts with a return and "hi / sirf / kata / maana": "Supplier ne kitna maana?" [₹300] [₹500]. The baaki changes by what the supplier accepted; the card says "₹500 ka maal, supplier ne ₹300 maana". With cash, question 2 follows |
| 4 | Replies feel slow | A new connection (TLS handshake) to WhatsApp for every reply; about 13 database trips per message; nothing on the phone while the bot works | "typing..." (and blue ticks) at once, finished before the reply goes; one kept-open connection to WhatsApp per event loop; every reply logged as "handled text in N ms (database Q queries D ms, whatsapp S sends W ms, other O ms)"; the live bot saves a `reply_timed` event and the daily health report shows the median and its parts. The region decision waits for these numbers on Render (step 7) |

Design rules behind the fixes: a word the bot does not know never becomes a card (it asks); the baaki changes by what
actually happened to the money (dues cut, or cash in hand), so the bot asks the one question that decides it, with the
usual answer first; speed is measured in parts before anything is moved.

**Measured on the laptop after the fixes (16 Nov, 21 replies, Round 18):** median reply 2.1 s (fastest 1.5 s, slowest
3.6 s). Per reply, the median parts: database 0.5 s (6 queries at about 84 ms each, laptop to Supabase Mumbai),
WhatsApp 0.6 s per message sent, other 1.0 s. "Other" was mostly waiting for Meta's typing call (0.7-1.2 s), so
"typing..." is now shown only for photos, voice notes and PDFs. The database is a third of each reply: that is the
region question, measured again on Render (step 7).
