# Problem Statement

## The problem (hypothesis, to be confirmed in discovery)
Small Indian shop owners receive several supplier bills a week, mostly on paper, sometimes handwritten. They record them by hand in a register, in an app built for typing, or not at all. The result: hours lost to data entry, missed or disputed supplier dues, and no clear view of cash.

## Evidence so far
- In 2,400+ SME product demos (Jan to Jul 2026), 69.6% named bill and accounts-payable processing as their top accounting pain, ahead of bank reconciliation (14%) and GST reconciliation (8.1%). See `03_market_facts.md`.

## Who
Owners of small shops with frequent supplier bills, 1 to 10 staff, where the owner handles accounts on a smartphone with WhatsApp. Segments to test: kirana and general stores, medical stores, hardware and sanitary shops.

## Candidate wedges (pick one after 15 interviews)
| Wedge | Problem |
|---|---|
| A. Supplier bills and payables (default) | Paper bills pile up; dues get missed or disputed |
| B. Customer udhaar reminders | Money owed by customers is hard to collect (crowded: Khatabook, OkCredit) |
| C. Daily cash and UPI matching | Owner does not know the true day's cash |

## Not solving
GST filing, payments or money movement, loans, open-ended chat.

## Success
15+ shops using it for 2+ weeks; measured time saved per bill; bill-reading accuracy on a fixed eval set; share of owners willing to pay.
